Five things for the board
  • Growth came from volume and returning students, not from price

    Weekly sales value doubled while the ticket rose from $926 to $974.

  • Existing students are now 44% of registrations

    The repeat buyer is the most valuable customer in the base. Protect the relationship and the list.

  • The attach rate at first sale is the single largest lever

    1.1 courses per new buyer against a 1.5 target is a 36% gap in revenue per buyer, at no acquisition cost.

  • Buying peaks in late summer and September, and troughs in early March

    Plan cohort starts and offers around August and September, and expect a thin first half of March.

  • The buyer is 55 plus, female, affluent and concentrated in four states

    A lifestyle purchase at full price. Sales hours, tone and scheduling should follow that profile and those time zones.

Sources and definitions

Weekly figures are the 39 Total rows for 26 January to 25 October 2026 in the French Atelier weekly funnel workbook (Funnel breakdown sheet), read on screen on 28 October 2026. Sales value is the workbook's revenue column, which equals registrations multiplied by the recorded average price per sale. It is sales value at registration, not processor settlement or refunds.

New buyers (acquisitions) are tracked in the workbook from 30 March 2026. Registrations from existing students are tracked separately from 7 September 2026; before that date all registrations sit in one column.

The buyer profile comes from the French Atelier client demographics board briefing (1 April to 31 July 2026, United States and Canada), built on Meta demographic breakdowns of the client base. Buyer level states, ages, courses and repeat purchases per person require the paid orders export from the eTeacher CRM, which this page does not include.
